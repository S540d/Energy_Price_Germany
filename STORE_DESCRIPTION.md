## Short Description (80 characters max)

## Full Description (4000 characters max)

**📊 Real-Time Energy Market Visualization**

Energy Prices Germany provides instant access to electricity market prices and renewable energy statistics for Germany. Perfect for energy-conscious consumers, solar panel owners, and anyone interested in Germany's energy transition.

**✨ Key Features:**

🔋 **Live Market Prices**
- Real-time electricity spot market prices (EPEX)
- Combined end-customer price view (market + grid fees/taxes)
- Price trends over 24-48 hours
- Color-coded visualization (green=low, red=high)

🌱 **Renewable Energy Share**
- Live percentage of renewable energy in the grid
- Track wind, solar, and other renewable sources
- Visualize Germany's energy transition progress

📈 **Correlation Analysis**
- See how renewable energy affects market prices
- Understand the relationship between supply and demand
- Time-of-day analysis (night, morning/evening, day)

🎨 **Modern User Interface**
- Clean, intuitive design
- Dark mode and system theme support
- Bilingual: English & German
- Responsive charts with interactive tooltips

🔒 **Privacy First**
- No user accounts required
- No personal data collection
- No ads or tracking
- Open source (MIT License)

**📱 Perfect For:**
- Homeowners with solar panels or battery storage
- EV owners optimizing charging times
- Energy-conscious households
- Students and researchers
- Anyone interested in Germany's Energiewende

**🌐 Data Source:**
All energy data is sourced from Energy Charts (Fraunhofer ISE), a trusted public research institution, and is licensed under CC BY 4.0.

**📊 Echtzeit-Energiemarkt-Visualisierung**

Energy Prices Germany bietet sofortigen Zugriff auf Börsenstrompreise und Erneuerbare-Energien-Statistiken für Deutschland. Perfekt für energiebewusste Verbraucher, Solaranlagen-Besitzer und alle, die sich für die deutsche Energiewende interessieren.

**✨ Hauptfunktionen:**

🔋 **Live-Marktpreise**
- Echtzeit-Börsen-Strompreise (EPEX Spot)
- Kombinierte Endkundenpreis-Ansicht (Markt + Netzentgelte/Steuern)
- Preistrends über 24-48 Stunden
- Farbcodierte Visualisierung (grün=niedrig, rot=hoch)

🌱 **Anteil Erneuerbarer Energien**
- Live-Prozentsatz erneuerbarer Energien im Netz
- Verfolge Wind, Solar und andere erneuerbare Quellen
- Visualisiere den Fortschritt der Energiewende

📈 **Korrelationsanalyse**
- Sieh, wie erneuerbare Energien die Marktpreise beeinflussen
- Verstehe den Zusammenhang zwischen Angebot und Nachfrage
- Tageszeit-Analyse (Nacht, Morgen/Abend, Tag)
